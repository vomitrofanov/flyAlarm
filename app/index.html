Which sites are flyable?

Decision support only, not a safety authority. Scores come from forecast models with simplified site rules, and models can underestimate wind at launch. Always check the sky, the windsock and local pilots before flying.

How to read a card: the blue fan is the direction the launch faces, the arrow is the forecast wind (green straight in, amber off axis, red outside the launch). Tap a card for details and tap an hour for its reason. “My sites” is stored on this device; use “copy link to my list” in the site picker to carry it to your phone.

Forecast data: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Flying sites: ParaglidingEarth community database, mirrored weekly. Town search: Open-Meteo geocoding.
